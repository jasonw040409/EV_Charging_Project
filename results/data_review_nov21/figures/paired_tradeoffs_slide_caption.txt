Each dot compares Combined with Power penalty for the same recorded session and assumed arrival SOC. Vertical values are Combined minus Power penalty; horizontal offsets separate individual cases and have no quantitative meaning. Black bars show median paired changes, not differences between unpaired medians. Negative exposure changes mean a smaller high-SOC score; positive peak/cost changes mean larger peaks/extra cost. Only complete clean pairs are shown (14, 14 and 9 at assumed 30%, 60%, 75% starts). The flagged original control and analytically infeasible cases are excluded from this figure, not erased from the study. SOC and prices are assumed/synthetic; repeated SOC scenarios are not independent sessions. Very small raw numerical differences remain in the plot but are not practically interpretable. These scheduling-proxy findings do not establish measured battery-health improvement; scientific review remains outstanding.
